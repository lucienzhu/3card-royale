# Rules · 3Card Royale

[简体中文](RULES.zh-CN.md) · [Play](https://lucienzhu.github.io/gk-royale-playtest/?lang=en)

Playtest version **0.3.8**. For two people and one standard 52-card deck, with no jokers.

## Set up

Use one standard 52-card deck without jokers. Both players start at level 2 with 8 private cards and no wounds. Randomly choose the first attacker; the winner starts the next game. Between games, gather and shuffle all 52 cards, clear wounds and deal 8 cards each. Only levels and failed-A lights carry over.

## Play cards & the flower card

Choose 3 cards from your hand and face-up wounds, or play 4 cards of one rank. Three-card and four-card plays may answer each other. The flower card is the wildcard. Both players share ♥2 first; after each game, the winner’s new level sets the shared heart wildcard for the next game. It stays fixed throughout that game, even when personal levels differ. A23 is a 3-high straight, QKA is A-high, and KA2 is not a straight. Suits have no ranking.

## Compare & resolve

Compare hand scores first, then ranks within the same type, from 2 low to A high. Three/four of a kind compare their shared rank; straights compare the top card (3 for A23); pairs compare the pair, then the kicker; flushes and high cards compare ranks from highest down. An attacking win deals the score difference in wounds; a defensive win earns that many swaps. A rank-only win gives 1 wound or 1 swap. An exact tie does nothing. Discard both plays and replace used wound cards face-up (attacker first), then resolve damage or swaps. New wounds are face-up. At 5 wounds you lose; count all damage when calculating level gains.

## Where you can swap

Spend 1 swap to give a card from your hand or wounds: trade blindly with a rival hand card, choose a rival face-up wound, trade with your own other zone, or discard and draw into the same slot. Hands stay private and wounds stay face-up. Zone sizes and wound counts do not change. You may end unused swaps early.

## Rounds, refills & the deck

Each player attacks once per round. Do not refill hands between the two attacks. At round end, keep unused cards and refill hands to 8. Alternate the first attacker each round, producing an A→B→B→A attack order. When the deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to continue.

## Level up & win at A

The game winner gains levels equal to the final wound-count difference, capped at A. Reach A, then win a game that starts at A to win the match. Each loss starting at A adds a red light. Three red lights reset only that player to level 2; the rival keeps their level. A win starting at A lights green and ends the match.

## Hand scores

Four of a kind **6** > Three of a kind **5** > Straight flush **4** > Flush **3** > Straight **2** > Pair **1** > High card **0**.

## All swap routes

| Give one of your cards from | Receive from |
| --- | --- |
| Hand | A random rival hand card / a chosen rival wound / your own wound / draw pile |
| Wounds | A random rival hand card / a chosen rival wound / your own hand / draw pile |

Every route costs one opportunity. Cards entering wounds are face up; cards entering hands remain private. Counts and damage never change during a swap. Direct exchanges do not draw cards. A draw-pile exchange discards the selected card, then fills its original slot. If the draw pile is empty, reshuffle discards; you may draw the card you just discarded.
