# Design language

[简体中文](DESIGN.zh-CN.md) · [Visual identity](https://lucienzhu.github.io/gk-royale-playtest/brand.html?lang=en)

The bougainvillea mark and playing-card outline connect a game made in Shenzhen to an ordinary deck. Three identical bracts rotate by 120°, each with a small golden flower. The mark’s geometry is retained.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Bougainvillea / fixed logo red | `#D83B74` |
| Flower center / fixed logo gold | `#DEB04A` |
| Paper | `#F7F7F2` |
| Sea mint | `#86D8C7` |
| Dusk background | `#111F2C` |

Logo red and gold are identical in light and dark themes. Surfaces, borders and functional text accents adjust for contrast. Use gold sparingly; flat color, open space and mint/blue surroundings carry the identity. These are design cues, not official city colors or demographic research.

Use the mark and wordmark as one aligned unit. Avoid a detached giant mark competing with the title. Use system fonts for UI and Arial/Helvetica with lining numerals for card ranks: 2–10 and JQKA share a baseline. Never use color as the only way to identify a state.

A single shared flower indicator belongs to rules 0.3.8. Historical variants retain separate player wildcards. Empty wound areas can collapse; a card’s meaning and available interactions must remain discoverable in the zone guide.

Keep the first action clear, reveal full rules on demand, and preserve the player’s selection when opening help. English is 3Card Royale; Simplified Chinese is 深圳花牌. Translation must preserve rules and distinguish a small game from the full race to Ace.

Original assets use MIT. Custom emoji PNGs are images for platforms that support uploads, not universal Unicode emoji. The mark is stylized and is not a claim of uniqueness or botanical realism.
